About Study Tracker

Study Tracker is designed to make consistent learning easier and less stressful. Whether you are preparing for exams, learning a new language, or improving a professional skill, Study Tracker helps you stay on track and understand how you are progressing.

Key Features

Session Tracking

Start a timer, choose your subject, and save your session with a single tap. Keep a clear record of how and when you study.

Goals and Streaks

Set daily, weekly, and monthly study goals. Build streaks that reward consistency and motivate you to keep going.

Progress Insights

Visual charts help you see your strongest days, understand your patterns, and decide where to adjust your routine.

Gentle Reminders

Receive soft, friendly reminders that encourage you to study without pressure, supporting a healthy and sustainable routine.

Notes and Reflections

Add brief notes after each session to capture what you learned, questions you had, or what you want to focus on next time.

Mascot Companion

A charming mascot celebrates your milestones, lightens the mood, and makes your study sessions feel less lonely.
