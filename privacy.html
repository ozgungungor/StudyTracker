Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 December 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the Study Tracker mobile application ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Study Tracker itself is designed to collect and store only minimal information needed for the app to function, such as:

  • Study sessions and related metadata (subject, duration, notes).
  • Basic app settings and preferences stored on your device.

We do not require you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal identifiers such as your name, address, or phone number.

2. Advertising and AdMob

Study Tracker uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app. AdMob may collect and use certain data in order to provide, personalize, and improve ads. This data may include:

  • Device information (such as device model, operating system, identifiers).
  • Approximate location information.
  • Interaction with ads (views, clicks, etc.).

Google may use the collected data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy and policy for partners .

3. Consent and Personalized Ads

Depending on your region, you may be asked for consent regarding personalized advertising. In such cases, you can allow or deny personalized ads through the consent dialog shown in the app.

You can also manage your ad preferences through your device settings or Google settings, where available.

4. How We Use Your Data

Data collected by the app is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide core study tracking features.
  • To store your study history, goals, and notes on your device.
  • To show ads via AdMob and support the development of the app.
  • To improve the app and fix bugs (for example, via anonymous crash or usage data, if enabled by your device or store provider).

5. Data Storage and Security

Study-related data (such as sessions and notes) is primarily stored locally on your device. Third-party services such as AdMob may store data on their own servers in accordance with their policies.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Study Tracker is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law for consent to online services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Your Choices

You can control certain privacy-related settings through:

  • Your device's operating system settings (for example, ad tracking preferences).
  • The consent dialog in the app (where applicable).
  • Managing or uninstalling the app from your device.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
